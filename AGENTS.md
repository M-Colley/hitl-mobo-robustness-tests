# Working in this repository

This file is for whoever works on the repository next, human or agent. `README.md` explains what the project is. This file explains how not to break it and where the traps are. Everything here was learned by hitting it.

## What the project claims

Bayesian optimization driven by human ratings is measured on twenty analytic landscapes with published optima, with four error processes injected at four magnitudes and two onsets. The deployed design's regret splits into search loss (the run never visited anything better) and selection loss (it visited something better and shipped the wrong one). Selection loss is zero under exact observation, so a noiseless benchmark cannot show it, and no acquisition function targets it directly. At 1σ from the first rating, the magnitude where the measured rater noise of the three human studies falls, selection is 41.8% [34, 50] of the deployed cost of error, rising to 83.1% [75, 90] at 5σ from trial 21. Pooled over all cells it is 61.8%, but that pool is a ratio of sums in which the 5σ cells carry 66% of the total, so it is close to the 5σ answer and is not the headline. No acquisition-side change tested improved the deployed design.

At 1σ from the first rating the gain of about a tenth belongs to a zero-trial ship rule: ship the visited design with the highest posterior mean less one latent SD (LCB1) after all trials. It recovers 10% [2, 18] of the deployed cost on the held-out seeds 12–16 (four processes pooled) and 14% [5, 21] on the untouched seeds 27–36 (Gaussian), but 4% [−2, 11] on seeds 7–11, at a price of 0.021–0.023 of the achievable improvement without error. A final sitting of two designs adds nothing measurable to it (+0.003 [−0.003, +0.009] held out, four processes; −0.008 [−0.017, +0.002] untouched, Gaussian). The final sitting's value lies where large error arrives mid-study: at 5σ from trial 21 a sitting of sixteen gains +0.227 [0.147, 0.321] of the achievable improvement held out (41% of the deployed cost, four processes pooled) and +0.249 [0.164, 0.339] over LCB1, which loses there, at a price of 0.033. Under Gaussian error alone its increment over LCB1 is +0.097 [0.030, 0.162] (Holm over k 0.12). Four confirmation trials cut false improvement claims from 15.7% to 0.5% (seeds 7–16).

For gross faults the largest recovery comes from a remedy that assumes nothing about the fault: refitting the surrogate on the ratings' ranks recovers 51% [36, 63] of the deployed cost (49% held out) at a price of 0.029 without error on those runs (0.024 held out, 0.027 on the main sweep), and it deserves the same caution as the cautious ship rule. Explicit models of the fault recover less: relevance pursuit 38% [24, 51] (price 0.011), per-rater offsets 32% [23, 40] of the cost of alternating raters (price 0.007), and clipping to the landscape's known range 22–44% depending on the tie rule (price zero by construction, because the clip acts only on noisy runs, never on the clean twin; its bounds use the known optimum, so it is not deployable as run).

Oracles fitted to archival-style ratings do not significantly understate the deployed loss in any model family: a tree ensemble reports 1.02 [0.88, 1.19] of the exact objective's deployed loss at 1σ from the first rating, as a share of the achievable improvement. What a fitted oracle reports of the search loss depends on its family. The case for exact objectives rests on the fitted oracle's misspecification, on that dependence on an arbitrary family choice and on how its achievable improvement is set (the landscape ranking moves with the family and the normaliser jointly), and on the verified optima and measurable geometry of the exact landscapes.

## Two metrics, never interchangeable

`simple_regret_true` is `y_opt − best_true_so_far`, the regret of the best design visited so far. It is search loss. The post-onset per-iteration AUC of its excess (`auc_simple_regret_excess_true_postonset_per_iter`) is the time-averaged search loss behind the dose-response, power-law, descriptor, frag and acquisition-ranking results. `inference_simple_regret_true` is the regret of the design with the best observed rating, which is what a study ships. At the final trial the shipped design's excess is typically two to three times the search excess. Name the metric and its time base in every sentence that quotes a number, and compare a final deployed value only with a final search value.

The standard ship rule breaks ties at the FIRST index: it ships the earliest design with the best observed rating (`np.argmax` in the simulator, `bo_sensor_error_simulation.py` near l.4271, and in `rescore_ship_rules.py`; stable sorts in `replay_end_of_study.py` and `replay_hitl_remedies.py`). Under continuous error a tie never matters: the main sweep's 407 tied noisy runs and 336 tied clean twins are all exact repeated ratings (in a clean twin, or before a trial-21 onset) of designs with the same true value, and the gross-fault arm has none that matter either. On a capped, clipped or rounded scale the top rating is tied between designs of different true value in most runs (fixed cap 99.5%, clipping 52–77%, instrument 61%), and the earliest tied design is the worse one in 53–70% of them. There the tie rule moves deployed numbers by tens of points, so give the uniform-random tie-break value beside every affected number: the capped scale's extra cost 185% [132, 258] against 135% [92, 196], the re-anchored cap 25% against 18%, the rank refit under the cap 36% against 23%, clipping at 15% of trials spiking at 20 SD 22% against 44%. `python scripts/review_checks/tie_break.py rescore` computes them.

## Non-negotiables

Score a process change against the standard process, never against its own clean twin. Replicating ratings, re-rating before deploying, or changing the acquisition also changes the clean run, so excess over the arm's own twin measures it against a handicapped reference. This once made replication look as if it recovered a third of the cost when it recovers nothing. `scripts/analyse_boba_adaptations.py` implements the right estimand (`cost = ref_noisy − ref_clean`, `gain = ref_noisy − trt_noisy`, `price = trt_clean − ref_clean`). Import `paired_frame` and `summarise` from it instead of writing a second copy. When the reference is a pooled mean over several acquisitions, say so, because a strong single acquisition then looks like a remedy.

Score a procedure that spends trials on identification against the standard process AND against the zero-trial ship rules on the same runs: the rule it builds on, and the strongest zero-trial rule chosen on the selection seeds, not the one that looks best on the test seeds. The final sitting ranks its candidates by LCB1 (posterior mean less one latent SD), but LCB2 (less two) is the rule seeds 7–11 pick at 1σ from the first rating and at 5σ from trial 21. At 1σ LCB1 after 50 trials gains almost as much as the sitting of two (+0.027 against +0.030 held out), so the sitting's own contribution is +0.003 [−0.003, +0.009]. At 5σ from trial 21 under Gaussian error LCB2 gains +0.145, and the sitting's advantage over it is only +0.065 [0.010, 0.122]. `sitting_by_magnitude.py` writes `inc_lcb1_*`, `inc_lcb2_*`, `inc_pm_*` and `increment_over_chosen_rule`.

Never report a recovery ratio whose reference cost is near zero or changes sign across cells. Report the absolute difference instead. `compare_boba_arms.py` suppresses `share_removed` when the pooled reference is below 0.01 of the achievable improvement or negative on any landscape, in cells, overall, per benchmark and per acquisition; a landscape whose reference is exactly zero does not suppress.

Report the price of every process change beside its recovery, and compare prices only across the same acquisitions. The cautious rule's 0.015 is over ten acquisitions; on LogEI and qNEI, where the replayed remedies (rank refit, shortlist, LUCB) run, it is 0.022 and the rank refit's is 0.027. LCB1 and LCB2 are not free for LogEI and qNEI (0.021–0.023 of `opt_z`, more than the two-design sitting's 0.015–0.018). A sitting's price depends on k only, because the clean twin is shared by every cell and process, and it is not monotone in k: 0.015, 0.010, 0.012, 0.015, 0.022, 0.028 for k = 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 16 on seeds 7–16. A price is `budget_split.py --twin clean` or the arm's `trt_clean − ref_clean`; never use the gain at 0.05σ as a price.

Never report an acquisition robustness ranking without naming the incumbent. Against its own clean twin, the observed-max incumbent removes 20.0% of the summed post-onset trajectory loss in `opt_z` units (15.6% in landscape SDs) and 40% for LogPI, and moves UCB and qNEI by exactly 0.000 because they never read `best_f`. That zero is the control that validates the pairing. Against the standard process it recovers 19% [10, 27] of the trajectory loss and makes the deployed design worse by 10% [5, 15] of its cost. Every ranking here ranks acquisition-and-incumbent pairs.

Do not re-raise the GP-noise confound. Supplying the true injected variance as `train_Yvar` removes 3.4% of the summed post-onset trajectory loss against its own clean twin in `opt_z` units (5.0% in landscape SDs); against the standard process it changes the cost by −3% on both the trajectory and the deployed design; nothing survives FDR correction, and the onset ratio does not shrink. The onset effect remains unexplained, and the text should say so.

`random` and `sobol` must show 0.000e+00 excess search regret under response error. They ignore observations, so any other number means the pairing is broken. Their deployed design is still chosen by noisy ratings, so they are no zero control on the deployed metric, and under input error they evaluate displaced points and are no zero control at all. A claim about the floors on the deployed design concerns their ABSOLUTE deployed regret (`review_checks/floor_deployed.py`, 2000 landscape resamples, Holm over the ten acquisitions): at 5σ from the first rating only LogPI beats the floor after Holm. `tab:acq`'s deployed column is the excess, on which the floors always look best: their search excess is exactly zero, so their deployed excess is selection loss alone, and that selection loss is at or near the top of the acquisitions' range (1σ from the first rating: 0.178 against 0.105 to 0.164; 5σ: 0.501 against 0.312 to 0.520). Their clean run already ships a poor design (0.348 of `opt_z`).

Aggregate as ratios of landscape means with a landscape bootstrap, never as means of per-run ratios. The suite's achievable improvement (`opt_z`) spans 74×, from 0.76 on `powell` to 56.8 on `shekel`, so per-run ratios let a landscape with tiny regret outvote one with large regret. The landscapes share their random numbers by design, so state that the bootstrap treats correlated clusters as independent. With three or four clusters (the fitted-oracle datasets, the halo problems), report the cluster count and the per-dataset values (`output-boba/analysis/adaptations_per_dataset.csv`), and do not star results: with three clusters the percentile bootstrap is exactly the min–max range, and the exact two-sided Wilcoxon cannot go below 2/2^n, 0.25 at three clusters and 0.125 at four.

A pooled onset ratio is a ratio of landscape means, so its denominator weights the landscapes: the scalar Gaussian 1σ ratio is 9.6× on the floor gap and 5.5× on `opt_z`. Compare arms on one basis and divide by the running-maximum bound (`onset_bound.py`; `review_checks/mo_onset_bound.py` for the multi-objective arm).

Name the unit of every regression and variance share. Every mediation R² in `mediator_model.csv` and `tab:mediation` is on `excess_sd`, the post-onset excess in landscape SDs; `mediator_model_normalised.csv` holds the `opt_z`-free rows on both units. frag alone beats the magnitude alone only in landscape SDs (0.52 against 0.16; on the fraction of `opt_z` 0.46 against 0.51), and on the fraction of `opt_z` frag adds to the magnitude only when it is left in landscape SDs (+0.022 [0.007, 0.066]; divided by `opt_z`, +0.002 [−0.018, +0.021]). Never refit the descriptor rows on fragility. `factor_variance_shares.csv` has a `response` column, and the order of the factors reverses between units: on the fraction of `opt_z` the magnitude leads (24%) and the landscape takes 10%; in landscape SDs the landscape leads (32%) and the magnitude takes 10%. `descriptor_vif.csv` holds the VIFs of the PRIMARY descriptor model's own design, indicators included (log `opt_z` 1.2), and is what `tab:descriptors` prints; the seven-descriptor VIFs (log `opt_z` 9.2, sparsity 5.8, skew 6.3) are in `descriptor_vif_all.csv`, and the 9.2 is quoted only as "with sparsity and skew added".

Name the Holm family with every adjusted p. `heldout_remedies.py` tests each procedure in its own scope (column `scope` of section `4_holm`). The fixed k, the derived budget rule and the best-arm (LUCB) sittings are only in the 41-member "all" family, not the 27-member core family, and the rank rules are tested on the main sweep, not on the spike and ceiling arms that give their headline recoveries. The one LUCB sitting that survives Holm, `lucb_k8_rho0.5`, survives because of the optimistic ρ = 0.5 sitting noise, not its allocation: at the same noise one look each to the top eight recovers more on seeds 7–16 (18.7% against 16.1%). Never quote its survival without that comparison. LUCB draws from the top 3k by lower bound (`pool_factor` 3), not the top k.

Fitted-oracle arms: every run seed refits the oracle (`bo_sensor_error_simulation.py`, `build_oracle(seed=seed)`), so `y_opt`, the model-free floor, the oracle's mean and `sigma_f` all differ between the seeds of one dataset or landscape. Form every normaliser within each seed and aggregate as mean_s(excess_s)/mean_s(normaliser_s); never take the maximum clean best over seeds (`oracle_isolation.seed_optima`/`floor_gap`, `analyse_fitted_companion.fitted_optimum`/`floor_gap`/`fitted_achievable`, `review_checks/oracle_achievable.seed_achievable`/`sigma_f_achievable`/`exact_best_clean`). Compare a fitted arm with the exact arm on the share of the achievable improvement, not on the floor gap: for the exact arm that is `opt_z`, for the fitted arm the seed's `y_opt` less that oracle's mean over the search box. The floor gap is 1.1–1.7% of `opt_z` on Branin, Powell and Rosenbrock, which is what carried the withdrawn "about a quarter" (a floor-gap ratio of landscape means, 0.20 [0.11, 0.62]), and a tree oracle's is inflated by an optimum a median 17% of its achievable improvement above the best clean value. Each family sets its own achievable improvement (a tree's `y_opt` − mean is a median 1.27 × `sigma_f` × `opt_z`, a GP's or MLP's about 1), so a claim about oracle families must hold under all four normalisers (primary, `best_clean`, `sigma_f`, `both_best_clean`); the closing table of `review_checks/oracle_families.py` shows them side by side. The tree's search-loss understatement (0.72 [0.56, 0.93]) is significant under only two of the four; the MLP reports more search loss than the trees under all four; on the deployed design the family contrasts change sign between normalisers at 1σ and 5σ. Write "similar median fidelity", never "equal fidelity".

`gp.likelihood.noise` is the noise variance of the standardised targets. Multiply it by `gp.outcome_transform.stdvs ** 2` before combining it with anything in objective units.

Centre variables before forming interaction or quadratic terms, otherwise the linear coefficient is a conditional slope at zero of the raw variable.

Record the command behind every table in `paper/COMMANDS.md`. A number without a recorded producer does not go into the paper.

## Traps that have cost real time

A run's variant lives only in its file name. One output directory can hold several spike sizes or two ceiling modes. `run_metadata.json` and the per-arm summary CSVs are overwritten by the last invocation, so they describe only the last variant. `evaluate_research_question.py` parses the suffix into a `variant` column and puts it in `CONDITION_COLS`.

The file-name suffix now also carries the rating instrument: `_clip<lo>,<hi>`, `_clip-sample` and `_round<q>`, as the last parts of the suffix and only when those flags are set, so standard runs keep their names. `output-boba-instrument` and `output-boba-spike-clip` hold clipped or rounded runs under STANDARD file names, because they ran before the name carried the instrument. Never resume them in place: `_guard_instrument_naming` refuses such a directory, so use a fresh `--output-dir`, and then update every analysis that pairs by name (`analyse_boba_adaptations.py`'s `spike-clip-*` arms need `variant=f"{_sp}_clip-sample"` with `ref_variant` staying `_sp`; the instrument arm's evaluation will carry the variant `clip-8,8_round0.55`; `make_boba_paper_tables.py` reads `output-boba-instrument/analysis`).

A spike run's sitting noise comes from its file-name variant `sp<p>-<size>`, where the size is the spike SD actually used, never from `run_metadata.json`. `build_tasks` puts a stem's single spike variant into the task's `Settings.spike_variant`, and `sitting_sd_fn` falls back on it when a caller passes no `variant=` (`replay_hitl_remedies.py` passes none); a stem that mixes spike variants raises. The SD-only path gets sqrt(σ² + p s²) = 7.75 for `sp0.15-20` (it was 0.268 before the fix, so the look-buying rows of `output-boba-spike/analysis/hitl_remedies{,_heldout}` on disk are stale; the corrected ones are in `hitl_remedies_spikesd{,_heldout}`). LUCB looks take only an SD, so under spikes they are Gaussian at the marginal SD. The capped-scale arm replays its looks without the cap, so its look-buying rows are optimistic in the same way; only its rows that buy no look are quoted.

A variant that changes the clean run needs its own `--output-dir`. The simulator's guard (`_clean_run_settings` in `bo_synthetic_error_simulation.py`) tracks only minimum distance, the input-uncertain acquisition, relevance pursuit, the rater model and the halo model. It does not track the Student-t likelihood, the noisy-input GP, `--replicate-first`, `--final-rerate` or `--inference-rule`, so check those by hand.

Rerunning an arm into its old directory reuses its old runs. The drivers skip a directory that contains `SWEEP_COMPLETE`, and every run uses `--resume`, which matches on file name only. Code changes therefore need a fresh directory.

A baseline's oracle name can contain an underscore (`extra_trees`). Parse it from the logged `oracle_model`, never by regex over the file name.

A run's `y_opt` is not a column of `paired_excess_metrics.csv`. It is `final_best_true_baseline + final_simple_regret_true_baseline`, constant within a seed; for the exact arm it equals `opt_z`, which `oracle_achievable.exact_best_clean` checks. An oracle's mean over the box can be estimated without refitting, from the designs of each seed's clean random and Sobol floor runs (`analyse_fitted_companion.box_means`); on the isolation arms that moves no ratio by more than 0.013.

The oracle-isolation error grids and `corr_oracle_truth` come from the seed-7 oracle only. The GP and MLP arms' other seeds differ in `sigma_f` by 0.57–1.98× (`review_checks/oracle_sigmaf_seeds.py`). A rerun should calibrate per seed or fix the oracle seed. Run the oracle checks in the order `oracle_sigmaf_seeds`, `oracle_achievable`, `oracle_families`: each reads the one before. With four acquisitions and twenty landscapes, per-landscape best-acquisition agreement is at chance even for the exact arm against itself across seed halves (16/60 on the trajectory, 17/60 on the deployed design), so do not cite it as evidence. Each oracle-isolation arm holds 2,400 run files (600 clean, 1,800 noisy) under `runs/`; the other CSVs there are summaries.

CatBoost writes `catboost_info/` into the working directory unless told not to. Oracles are now built with `allow_writing_files=False` (fits are bit-identical), and in-process fits of the oracle-isolation pipeline go through `oracle_isolation.fit_outside_repo`. The tracked `catboost_info/` folder is a leftover.

`analyse_boba_adaptations.paired_frame` raises for a dataset without a scale (`KeyError`) or with a non-positive one (`ValueError`); it no longer falls back to 1.0. Every `ARMS` entry names its scale with `arm(..., scale=...)`: `opt_z`, `hv_floor_gap` (the multi-objective floor gap, `analyse_boba_mo.achievable_gain` on `output-boba-mo`) or `fitted_achievable` (`analyse_fitted_companion.fitted_achievable` on `output-fitted`), and `dataset_scale(kind)` computes each. A caller that passed `{}` for a relative arm, such as `review_checks/tie_break.py`, must pass `aba.dataset_scale(spec['scale'])`. The pooled BH family in `adaptations_recovery.csv` spans every arm, so rescaling or adding one arm moves the other arms' pooled q values, but never their recovery or intervals.

The tie-break check has two steps. `tie_break.py scan` reads every per-run log (about 15 minutes on 6 workers) and writes `review/tie_break/tie_runs.parquet`, which is git-ignored; `tie_break.py rescore` reads only that parquet. After any new run logs, rerun the scan first.

`replay_end_of_study.py --sitting-process shared` (the default) is the model of every sitting number in the paper: a simultaneous sitting whose bias, drift ramp and AR(1) state cancel. `--sitting-process sequential` continues the ramp and the AR(1) state (from the logged `error_magnitude` at trial T − k) over trials T − k + 1 … T and changes only the drift and AR(1) rows; `--sitting-order rank` shows the candidates best-ranked first. Each needs its own `--output-dir`. `settings.json` records `sitting_process` and `sitting_order` only when they are not the default, so old directories still match their settings. `replay_hitl_remedies.py` has no `--sitting-process`, so the LUCB sittings exist under the shared model only. `review_checks/sitting_sequential.py` is the process sensitivity; its shared side must reproduce the published outputs before it prints a sequential value, and it exits non-zero otherwise, so rerun it after any change to `sitting_by_magnitude.py`, `heldout_remedies.py` or their inputs.

`sitting_by_magnitude.py --replay-dirs` takes `dir=drift+ar1` to read only those error processes from a directory, and filtered entries must partition the runs; every tagged run records its replay directories in a `provenance` entry of its selection JSON. Where one quantity is printed by two generators, the means agree and the intervals differ in the third decimal (the first and reference blocks of `fresh_seed_replication.txt`; the selection JSON and the per-k CSV rows of `sitting_by_magnitude`). Quote one source consistently: the paper quotes the selection JSON (which the table prints), the per-k CSV for a k that was not chosen, and the first block of the fresh check for the sitting's gain.

When adding quantities to an existing producer, draw them from a new generator created after every existing draw, and test that the old outputs are unchanged by monkeypatching the new feature off (`tests/test_sitting_by_magnitude.py::test_the_other_rules_leave_every_earlier_output_unchanged`). Compare NaN-bearing summaries as JSON text, not with `==`.

`make_boba_paper_tables.py` writes every generated file in `paper/tables/` except `sitting_by_magnitude.tex`, which `scripts/sitting_by_magnitude.py` writes by default, and the hand-written caption wrapper `benchmarks_wrapper.tex`. It stops with exit 1, naming the table and the file, when an input of any table `main.tex` inputs is missing; `--allow-missing` keeps the old table instead and is only for a partial checkout. No table reads a `run_metadata.json`. The matched reference rows, `review_checks/c6_ratio.py`, `oracle_companion_estimators.py` and `mo_onset_bound.py` take `opt_z` from `boba_landscape_stats.json`; `instrument_scale.py` still reads `output-boba-instrument/run_metadata.json`, and `mo_onset_bound.py` the multi-objective arm's, through `analyse_boba_mo.achievable_gain`. That table sets `\tabcolsep` to 3pt itself, so its nine columns fit the TMLR text width. `check_paper.py` reads a tabular spec only up to its first closing brace, so a spec containing `@{}` counts zero columns.

`budget_split.py`'s defaults (one replay directory, grid 2..12, so n0 = 38) are not the paper's study. The paper reads `--ksweep-dir output-boba/analysis/end_of_study_ksweep,output-boba/analysis/end_of_study_kwide --k-grid 2,3,5,8,12,16,20,25,30`. Every variant needs `--output-suffix` (`_rho0.5`, `_decision_sequential`, `_kmax25`, `_clean`); `--rate-source truth` and `--twin clean` refuse to run without one. The derived rule never ships the LCB design: both no-trial rules are scored by the posterior mean of the design they ship, so the posterior-mean pick always scores at least as high, and its posterior-mean share says nothing about trust in the posterior.

Never edit a script while a `ProcessPoolExecutor` run of it is in progress. On Windows the workers re-import the module when they spawn, so a mid-run edit can mix two code versions in one output. A run whose workers died with `BrokenProcessPool` can be rerun with the same command: outputs are seeded per run and do not depend on the worker count.

The standard acquisitions ignore `--acq-raw-samples`. `get_botorch_candidate` passes the top `acq_num_restarts` (10) of a `candidate_pool` (1,000) uniform screen as `batch_initial_conditions`, so BoTorch generates no raw-sample restarts; only the one-shot qKG path uses `raw_samples` = 512 (with 8 fantasies). `xi` is added to `best_f` in objective units, because `Standardize` returns the posterior in training-target units.

The noise seed depends on the position of the acquisition in `ACQUISITION_CHOICES` and of the error model in `ERROR_MODEL_CHOICES`. Append new names at the end and never insert. `tests/test_acquisition_seed_order.py` and `tests/test_error_model_labels.py` pin the order. The main sweep's `random` and `sobol` runs, the floors of `output-boba-extensions` and `output-boba-ladder`, and all of `output-boba-mo` ran under the order used before `qkg` and `replei` were inserted, and reproduce exactly only under it (`check_provenance.ACQUISITION_ORDER_BEFORE_ROBUST` reconstructs it). A rerun under the current order is a different noise draw: the floors' search trajectory is unchanged, but their deployed design (the argmax of noisy ratings) changes.

In the main sweep the bias and Gaussian arms draw independent noise streams, because the jitter `SeedSequence` includes the error-model index. The seed omits the landscape, so every landscape sees the same stream for a given acquisition and seed. The onset-0 difference between the two arms is therefore sampling noise; `bias_onset0_control.csv` gives landscape-, stream- and two-way clustered SEs (0.013 to 0.038).

The recorded `git_commit` of an arm run before 2026-09-30 does not identify its code. The evidence is `scripts/check_provenance.py`, reported in `output-boba/analysis/review/provenance_check*.{csv,md,stamp.json}`, one run per arm: 64 of 71 BO runs are exact from their record, 7 are exact only under the old acquisition order or the as-logged dataset configuration, the comparison loop's old log is not reproduced, and its rerun (`output-elicitation-rerun`, `check_provenance.ELICITATION_ARM`) is, 40 of 40 clean seed-7 runs. The check ran on the working tree of 2026-09-30 (commit 736d91bd3 plus an uncommitted diff whose sha256 is in the `.stamp.json`), not on a commit; rerun it after the tree is committed. `code_stamp` now records `code_diff_sha256` and `dirty_files` per invocation and writes the patch, home directory masked, to the git-ignored store `output/code_diffs/` (or `$HITL_CODE_DIFF_DIR`), never into an output directory, because a patch carries diff context and the anonymous mirror would serve it. `tests/test_regression_logged_run.py` pins a default main-sweep run (branin, LogEI, seed 7, Gaussian 1σ from trial 21, and its clean twin) exactly against its logged CSV; a code change that moves any logged number fails it, and it skips where `output-boba` is absent.

The comparison loop's log (`output-elicitation/elicitation_runs.csv`) is not regenerated by the committed `scripts/elicitation_compare.py`: the command once recorded ran every landscape of `boba_landscape_stats.json` (29), the log has 20, and on the shared landscapes 39 of 40 clean seed-7 runs differ. Its numbers come from a rerun into `output-elicitation-rerun` with the twenty names written out so that the record names its landscapes itself (`paper/COMMANDS.md`, Section 8; `check_provenance.py` reads the command from that line and resolves `--functions` as the script does); never quote the old log. BoTorch's `PairwiseGP` draws from numpy's global generator at every fit (the Laplace start), and `fit_gpytorch_mll`'s retries draw from torch's, so any loop that uses them must seed both global generators per run, or no two executions agree. `review_checks/elicitation_determinism.py` reruns 96 of the rerun's runs in another worker schedule and compares every column but the wall-clock seconds.

Remote datasets are pinned by `data_commit` in `datasets*.json`. `fetch_remote_dataset` clones at the pin and refuses a cached clone at another commit unless `HITL_DATA_COMMIT_MISMATCH=warn`; it checks HEAD only, not a dirty working tree of the cache. The as-logged fitted arms (`output-fitted-prefix`, `-noaug-prefix`, `-adapt-rep10-prefix`) reproduce only with `datasets.json` at 3d2323aed~1 (`check_provenance --dataset-config-override 'output-fitted*-prefix/*=git:3d2323aed~1'`); their `run_metadata.json` records `objective_map` but not `column_ranges` or `oracle_target`. The tracked `archival_error_processes.*` files come from the pre-fix inputs (`datasets.json` at 3d2323aed~1 plus `output/pre_fix_2026-09-23/*`); run with its defaults on the current `datasets.json`, the estimator changes the opticarvis and provoice rows.

The `run_metadata.json` of the git-ignored arms (`output-boba`, `-extensions`, `-knownnoise`, `-incumbent`, `-ladder`, `-mo`, `-budget25`, `-budget100`, `-instrument`, `-robust`, `-confirmatory`) record absolute local paths (`stats_path`, `landscape_stats_path`, `mo_stats_path`). Never force-add one without first rewriting those fields to repository-relative paths; the tracked arms' files already carry relative ones. Never pass a directory to `git add -f`: it adds every ignored file under it, run logs, these `run_metadata.json` files and `tie_runs.parquet` included. Add a directory with plain `git add` and force-add only named files.

`design_rules_from_pilot.py` reads the whole capped-arm evaluation, which now also holds seeds 12–16. The published instrument screen (`design_rule_instrument_screen.csv`: 0.219, Spearman 0.65) reproduces only with `--ceiling-seeds 7,8,9,10,11`; without it the script gives 0.242 and 0.58. Its uncapped side is the main sweep's cell means over ten acquisitions and all seeds, not the capped runs' own pairs; `tie_break.py rescore` gives both that estimand and a paired version under both tie rules.

`output-boba-slip-mislabelled` holds runs with jittered names that were logged with `error_model` "none" (the corrupted first input-error arm). A naive scan files them as baselines. Nothing in `scripts/` or `paper/` reads that directory.

The input arms' (slip, misclick) sitting uses an assumed `--slip-look-sd` of 0.25 in both twins, because the rating there is exact. Quote a slip or misclick sitting number with that assumption; the sensitivity is in `end_of_study_slipsd*` (slip 31.9% at SD 0 to 17.8% at 0.5, prices 0.012 to 0.030).

`changepoint_compare.py` re-tunes its own CUSUM (κ grid 0.25–2, no window) and picks κ 0.25. The freeze rule's detector is κ 16, h 2000 (`stopping_tuned_A.json`), and w only picks the refit prefix. `changepoint_freeze_point.csv` scores that fixed point and checks it against rule A's logged alarms (0 mismatches on 13,200 streams). `stopping_summary.csv`'s `false_alarm_rate` is per run with error, with onset-0 and late-onset runs pooled; no late-onset run alarms before its onset on the held-out seeds, so the rate on runs with error from trial 1 is twice the pooled rate (6.6% against 3.3% held out; 7.5% on clean runs). The freeze rule's price without error is 0.028 of `opt_z` on the deployed design, 0.007 of it from stopping.

`analyse_extra_runs` counts extra trials from `origin`, the trial at which the clean twin itself first came within the tolerance of its k-trial regret, not from k. The noisy run's reach trial is origin + extra, and the per-run `multiplier` column is (k + extra)/k, which is not reach/k. At k = 25 and 1σ the origin is median 18, so a median extra of 41 and a median reach trial of 56 are consistent. Do not add k to the extra.

The multi-objective headroom screen is 0.15 (`analyse_boba_mo.HEADROOM_MIN`); the scalar screen is 0.10. The archival instruments' geometry is in `output-boba/analysis/review/instrument_scale.csv`: composite grids of 0.14/0.05/0.37 `sigma_f`, ceilings 2.4–3.8 `sigma_f` above the average design, at about the optimum; provoice's span is a lower bound. The instrument arm's ±8σ clip and 0.55σ step are a design choice, not a measurement, and the clip's top lies above the optimum on 17 of the 20 landscapes.

Response clipping's zero price is structural. `resolve_clip('sample')` bounds noisy ratings to [minimum of the 65,536-point Sobol stats sample, `y_opt`], which requires knowing where the objective's range ends, and clean twins never reach `_postprocess_response`. Describe the remedy as clipping to the landscape's known range, never to the observed range.

The spike (gross-fault) and ceiling (capped-scale) arms have seeds 12–16. Their replays live in `analysis/hitl_remedies_heldout`, separate from `analysis/hitl_remedies` (seeds 7–11), and `heldout_remedies.py` reads only the latter.

Bash heredocs with an unquoted delimiter eat backslashes. Use a quoted delimiter or a file editor for LaTeX, regexes and Windows paths.

`nohup bash -c '...' &` does not survive the tool call that started it, while `nohup python ... &` does. Background jobs also die when the machine sleeps. Always use `--resume`, into the right directory.

The simulation machine is a Windows box with 24 usable workers. The drivers read `$env:PYTHON` and otherwise use `$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\Python\Python312` (Python 3.12.9, torch 2.13.0+cu126, BoTorch 0.18.2.dev23 at commit eea35f51a, gpytorch 1.15.2). torch there is a CUDA build, so do not install `requirements-eval.txt` over it: the file documents that machine's environment for a fresh install elsewhere, and pip would rebuild BoTorch from git and reinstall statsmodels over its broken dist-info (linear_operator 0.6.1 and statsmodels 0.15.0 are pinned from the installed environment, because no run records them). The simulator never uses the GPU. A run costs about 21.5 worker-seconds, and the main sweep costs about 470 worker-hours. Move analysis files between machines, not simulations.

The main sweep (`output-boba/`) and several other arms keep their run logs only on that machine. Their analysis directories are tracked; the per-landscape `evaluation/paired_excess_metrics.csv` files the tables read are tracked by force-adding them, because the directory patterns in `.gitignore` hide their parents. Back the logs up separately.

`tests/test_hierarchical_oracle.py` needs jax and numpyro, which the module under test imports at import time. A module-level `pytest.importorskip("numpyro")` turns a missing install into a skip of that one file; before it was added, pytest abandoned the whole run at collection and reported nothing.

Smoke-test a driver before an overnight run. `run_boba_budget_neutral.ps1 -Smoke` runs every variant of every arm at toy size. It has caught an arm silently skipped by a `--help` guard and an arm whose two variants collided in one directory.

A copied paper directory's `main.aux` and `main.bbl` can be stale, and a copy without `figures/*.pdf` compiles one page long. Build page counts from a clean copy that includes the figure PDFs, with `*.aux`, `*.bbl` and `*.out` removed. From a clean directory, two `pdflatex` passes after `bibtex` still leave "Label(s) may have changed"; run a third, or use `paper/build_overleaf_bundle.py`, which repeats `pdflatex` until the log asks for no rerun.

## What is settled, and what is not

An arm that replaces the acquisition is scored against LogEI, never against the mean of the ten standard acquisitions: that reference carries the four weakest arms' cost and produced a recovery above 100% in one cell. The `*-ten` companions keep the other version visible.

Settled, with the measurement. Selection is 41.8% [34, 50] of the deployed cost of error at 1σ from the first rating and 83.1% [75, 90] at 5σ from trial 21. No acquisition-side change tested improved the deployed design, and augmented EI does not improve the trajectory either once it is scored against LogEI (+1% [-18, +18]). Early replication and Thompson sampling make the deployed design worse.

The end of the study. At 1σ from the first rating the zero-trial LCB1 rule does the work: 10% [2, 18] of the deployed cost held out (four processes pooled), 14% [5, 21] untouched (Gaussian), 4% [−2, 11] on seeds 7–11, price 0.021–0.023. A final sitting of two adds +0.003 [−0.003, +0.009] held out and −0.008 [−0.017, +0.002] untouched (Gaussian); its gain over the standard process (+0.030 [0.012, 0.050], 12% [5, 18], four processes held out) survives Holm over the nine sizes, and its increment over either zero-trial rule survives no correction. At 5σ from trial 21 a sitting of sixteen gains +0.227 [0.147, 0.321] held out (41% of the deployed cost, four processes pooled) and +0.249 [0.164, 0.339] over LCB1, and it holds when the drift ramp and the AR(1) state move through the sitting (+0.226 with the candidates in random order, +0.212 in rank order); the advantage comes mostly from drift, AR(1) and bias, and under Gaussian error alone the increment over LCB1 is +0.097 [0.030, 0.162] (Holm over k 0.12). On the untouched seeds a sitting of twelve beats LCB1 by +0.111 [0.048, 0.174] in that cell (Gaussian). Always name the scope (four processes pooled or Gaussian; seeds 7–11, 12–16 or 27–36; held out or untouched) and the price.

The cautious ship rule (LCB2) recovers 7.7% [2.7, 12.8] at 1σ over both onsets and breaks even between 0.25 and 1σ; its pooled 10.1% [4.6, 15.9] (seeds 7–16, ten acquisitions, price 0.015) draws two thirds of its cost from the 5σ cells. Four confirmation trials cut false improvement claims from 15.7% to 0.5%, but the standard process runs no test at all, so the trials take it to 6.6% and the alpha = 0.05 test, which costs no trial but cuts detection of real improvements from 88% to 51%, does the rest.

The model-based budget rule decides once, at n0 = T − max k = 20. At the late onset it is blind by construction (its fit is identical at all four magnitudes), and its whole held-out shortfall to k = 12 lies there (−0.051 [−0.071, −0.030] at onset 20, almost all of it in the 5σ cell); without the 5σ cell from trial 21 it is level with k = 12 (0.000 [−0.014, +0.013]). Its price without error is 0.022 held out (k = 12: 0.024). The misspecified-posterior explanation is not supported: the late-onset shortfall appears on the six laws as well. The sequential recomputation (`--decision sequential`, a decision at each T − k) was built post hoc and is outside the Holm families; on all seeds it gains +0.009 [−0.003, +0.021], at a price of 0.017 [0.006, 0.031], and it still trails k = 12 from trial 21, partly because its search term reads rating error as progress. `scripts/review_checks/budget_split_by_onset.py` has the numbers.

Faulty raters. Refitting on the ratings' ranks recovers 51% [36, 63] of the cost of gross faults (49% held out) at a price of 0.029 on those runs (0.027 on the main sweep) and assumes nothing about the fault. Relevance pursuit recovers 38% [24, 51] (price 0.011), per-rater offsets 32% [23, 40] of the cost of alternating raters (price 0.007), and clipping to the known range 22% (44% with random ties) at a price of zero by construction. Under a saturating scale the remedies recover about a fifth with random ties (re-anchored cap 18%, rank refit under the cap 23%; 25% and 36% under the first-index rule). Detect-and-freeze costs 0.028 without error. The halo backfit is not a demonstrated remedy: 24% of halo error's deployed loss in hypervolume-floor-gap units, positive on all four multi-objective problems (+1% to +51%), −3% on the trajectory, seeds 7–16, and four problems allow no test (minimum p 0.125). Do not write "modelling a known fault recovers about half".

The fitted oracle. No family's fitted oracle significantly understates the deployed loss: the tree ensemble reports 1.02 [0.88, 1.19] of it at 1σ from the first rating as a share of the achievable improvement, and no family's deployed interval lies below 1 in any cell under any of the four normalisers. The model family changes the reported search loss (tree 0.72 [0.56, 0.93], significant under two of four normalisers; the MLP reports more than the trees under all four). The companion arm's "a third to a half" holds only as a fraction of the floor gap; as a share of the achievable improvement the fitted arm reports 11.9% against the analytic arm's 14.8%.

A comparison loop is immune to shared monotone faults such as drift by construction; a cap is monotone but not strictly, so two designs above it tie, which is why it does not help against a saturating scale. Its measured numbers come from the rerun of Section 8 of `paper/COMMANDS.md`. The CUSUM matches the classical optimal detector, though the two fire together on all but one held-out run because the residual is far from standard normal, so that comparison shows little; at the freeze rule's own operating point (κ 16, h 2000) the CUSUM detects 41.5% of held-out changes against the GLR's 41.4% at a 6.6% false-alarm rate. On the hypervolume floor gap neither the multi-objective nor the scalar arm is clearly the more fragile, and the multi-objective arm's flatter onset ratio is largely the running-maximum bound's.

Not settled. The human is simulated. The best fitted oracle has held-out R² of about 0.55, and the provoice oracle is worse than a constant. The oracle-isolation error grids were calibrated on the seed-7 oracle, while the smooth families' `sigma_f` varies 0.57–1.98× across seeds; a per-seed calibration needs 7,200 new runs. The noise-aware ship rules and the end-of-study procedures pay a fixed price whether or not error is present (LCB1 and LCB2 0.021–0.023, the sitting of two 0.015–0.018), so they cost more than they recover at small error; the cautious rule breaks even between 0.25 and 1σ. Response clipping has no price by construction, and a clip to the running range of observed ratings, the version a study could run, has not been simulated. The onset effect is unexplained. The untouched-seed test of the large sitting used k = 12, because sizes above twelve were never replayed on seeds 27–36. Order effects beyond the simulated processes (contrast, anchoring, fatigue beyond the drift ramp) are untested, and the best-arm sittings exist under the shared sitting model only. The rank rule's family-wise test in the scope of its recoveries (the gross-fault and capped-scale arms on seeds 12–16, `heldout_remedies.py` section 4b) exists only with the earliest tied design shipped; no test was run with random ties at the cap. The provenance check ran on an uncommitted tree and must be rerun after the commit. The replication planned in advance is void on its own control rule, and the post-hoc sensitivity analysis clears all four thresholds.

## Before you claim a result

1. Run `python -m pytest tests/ -q` and keep the log.
2. Run `python scripts/check_paper.py --paper paper`. It checks inputs, references and table shapes, and it does not compile the document.
3. Compile the paper (`pdflatex`, `bibtex`, then `pdflatex` three times from a clean directory). It is a TMLR submission in the official TMLR style: no page limit, but an unusually long main text slows the review, so check where the main text through Limitations ends. `python paper/build_overleaf_bundle.py` does this in a clean directory, collects the figures, repeats `pdflatex` until the log asks for no rerun, and prints the length; the zip it writes is the Overleaf upload.
4. Regenerate tables with `python scripts/make_boba_paper_tables.py --analysis output-boba/analysis` after any analysis change, and `python scripts/sitting_by_magnitude.py` after any change to the sitting's inputs (it writes `paper/tables/sitting_by_magnitude.tex`). Never edit generated files in `paper/tables/`.
5. Regenerate figures with `python scripts/make_paper_figures.py` after any analysis change, and look at the PNGs it writes beside the PDFs before committing.
6. Record every new command in `paper/COMMANDS.md`, in run order, with the outputs it writes.
