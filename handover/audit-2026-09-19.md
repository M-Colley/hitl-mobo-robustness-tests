# Code audit, 19 September 2026

Six parallel audits of the analysis code, each on one axis where an unfair
comparison had already occurred once in this project, plus my own pass over the
new abstract and the four figures. Every number below was recomputed
independently from the shipped CSVs; nothing here is taken on an agent's word.

Severity: **BLOCKER** changes a number in the abstract or invalidates a claim.
**MAJOR** changes a printed number or a caption. **LATENT** is a foot-gun that
has not fired in the shipped data.

## A. Four blockers that change the abstract

### A1. Augmented EI's "+41%" is an artefact of its reference arm

`analyse_boba_adaptations.py:129-134`. The arm `q-aei` is scored against
`ref_acqs = logei,ei,pi,ucb,qucb,qnei,logpi,qei,qpi,greedy`, the mean of the ten
standard acquisitions, four of which Table 4 ranks bottom. Augmented EI's own
acquisition is on neither side. Recomputed with the module's own
`load`/`paired_frame`/`summarise`, gaussian, seeds 7-11, pooled:

| reference | trajectory | deployed |
|---|---|---|
| the ten, as published | **+40.9% [+27, +53]** | +2.0% [-6, +10] |
| LogEI only | **+1.3% [-18, +18]** | -5.4% [-21, +9] |
| EI only | +3.6% [-17, +19] | **-14.6% [-24, -6]** |
| the six strong arms | +12.0% [-4, +29] | -0.5% [-9, +8] |

The reference is not merely generous, it is invalid: per cell the same arm
"recovers" +373% at 0.05 sigma onset 21, which would mean the arm under error
beats the standard process with no error at all.

Affected: abstract; `main.tex:496-499` ("+41% [+27,+53], the best of any arm we
ran"); `main.tex:492`; the top row of the budget-neutral table. The paper
discloses the ten-acquisition reference for qKG and replication
(`main.tex:1795-1796`) and **not** for augmented EI or Thompson sampling.

Note the paper's larger claim gets stronger, not weaker: against EI, augmented EI
is significantly **worse** on the deployed design. What evaporates is the
consolation that it at least improves the trajectory.

### A2. The k-curve's "+0.05" is the half-noise sitting, and the paper never says so

`replay_end_of_study.py:110` runs `rhos = (1.0, 0.5)`; `budget_split.py:96` takes
`--rho` default **0.5** and `:378` filters the k-sweep to it. rho is the share of
the idiosyncratic SD that survives a comparative sitting. rho = 1 is the paper's
own stated mechanism, shared error cancels and the rest does not; rho = 0.5
halves the remainder as well, and nothing justifies it. Appendix L says both are
reported. Every quoted number is the half one. Recomputed from the pooled
k-sweep, tournament regret in opt_z units against a standard of 0.3417:

| | rho = 0.5 (quoted) | rho = 1.0 |
|---|---|---|
| best k | 16 | **8** |
| regret at the best k | 0.2908, gain **+0.051** | 0.3200, gain **+0.022** |
| k = 16 | +0.051 | +0.022 |
| turnover point | about T/3 | about T/6 |

At 1 sigma alone under rho = 1 the curve rises monotonically after k = 3, so the
paper's recommendation of k about T/3 is worse than doing nothing at the
magnitude the paper itself calls realistic.

Affected: abstract; `main.tex:516-523`; `fig:kcurve`; the end-of-study recoveries
at `main.tex:543-545` (19.7% becomes 12.2%, 33.6% becomes 26.3%, 27.9% becomes
23.8%).

### A3. "false claims from 16% to 0.5%" compares no test with a test

`replay_end_of_study.py:442-450`:

```python
pick  = int(np.argmax(run.observed))
c_row = int(np.argmax(run.observed[:initial]))
claim = bool(run.observed[pick] > run.observed[c_row])
```

`pick` is the argmax over all T trials, so the claim is true unless `pick ==
c_row`. The standard process therefore claims almost always: its power is
1.000000 in all 24 cells and its size is 0.90 to 0.98. It is not a hypothesis
test. The contrast is "no test" against "a 5% test", not "with and against
without confirmation trials". The arm that separates the two effects is already
in the code but unreported:

| gaussian, 4800 runs each | claim rate | false claims |
|---|---|---|
| standard | 98.3% | 15.7% |
| fresh re-rating, no t-test | 81.1% | 6.6% |
| confirm_k2, with the t-test | 43.3% | 0.5% |

The four trials buy 15.7 to 6.6. The test, which costs nothing, buys 6.6 to 0.5.
More than half the headline drop is not what the trials bought.

Related, same appendix: `confirm_k2`'s power is 87.5% at 0.05 sigma and **6.8%**
at 5 sigma onset 0. The quoted pooled 50.7% is carried by the easy cells. At the
one magnitude where the false-claim problem is real the arm detects fewer than
one real improvement in ten. All six numbers in that paragraph are the gaussian
cells presented as if they covered four processes.

### A4. "explains half the variation in cost" is measured on a different response

`analyse_boba_robustness.py:826` fits `excess_sd`, the post-onset excess in
landscape SD units. The abstract calls the response "cost" after defining cost as
a fraction of achievable improvement. opt_z spans 74x. Reproduces the paper's
0.520 exactly on 640 cells:

| predictor | R2 on excess_sd | R2 on the fraction |
|---|---|---|
| indicators only | 0.040 | 0.216 |
| log sigma_e | 0.158 | **0.510** |
| frag | **0.520** | 0.462 |

On the response the abstract names, knowing the error magnitude alone explains
more than frag does. The claim holds on excess_sd and reverses on the fraction.

Same defect in the figure. The annotated Spearman 0.87 is nearly reproduced by
the magnitude alone (0.85 on the same 80 cells), because frag is monotone in
sigma_e. Within a magnitude, which is the "which landscapes are fragile"
question, the log-log correlation is +0.40, +0.48, +0.50, +0.51.

**This one also carries good news.** Register item E9 asks for the missing
baselines. On excess_sd they are 0.040 for the indicators and 0.158 for the
magnitude, so frag adds 0.36. Leave-one-landscape-out puts frag at 0.437 against
the descriptors' 0.407, so out of sample frag is ahead by 0.030 rather than
behind by 0.006 as `main.tex:897-899` says. Add both rows to the mediation table.

## B. Two captions I wrote on 18 September are wrong

### B1. `fig:dose`: "The gap between the lines is the selection loss"

The blue line is the post-onset **time average** of search loss; selection loss is
defined against the **final** search loss. The gap overstates it:

| cell | figure gap | true selection loss |
|---|---|---|
| 1 sigma, onset 1 | 15.61 pp | 12.43 pp |
| 0.25 sigma, onset 1 | 3.72 pp | 2.87 pp |
| 5 sigma, onset 1 | 49.13 pp | 42.60 pp |

Overstatement runs 7% to 30%. `main.tex:248` pledges that a final value is
compared only with a final value, and the Table 1 caption warns the two blocks
are not comparable. Delete the sentence or plot the final search loss.

### B2. `fig:kcurve`: "magnitudes of 0.25 sigma and above"

`budget_split.py` filters family, candidates, winner, rho, acquisitions and error
models, and no magnitude. The pool is all four magnitudes; 25% of the 12,800 runs
are at 0.05 sigma. Under the caption's own filter the headline would be +0.076,
not +0.051. The "four error processes" half of the caption is right. Fix the
caption, not the run.

## C. Majors that change a printed number

- **Arm contrasts are not in the paper's currency.** `compare_boba_arms.py:113,146`
  build `share_removed` from raw landscape-SD values; the comment says the
  contrast "does not need" an opt_z. `main.tex:255-260` promises every aggregate
  is a ratio of landscape means in opt_z units. Recomputed: incumbent at 1 sigma
  onset 0 goes +12.0% to **+24.0%**, pooled 15.6% to 20.0%; known noise at 0.05
  sigma onset 0 goes -18.9% to **+5.1%**, a sign flip. "Two thirds or more at 0.25
  sigma and below" becomes about a half to three quarters.
- **The interaction coefficient has the wrong sign.** `analyse_boba_robustness.py:852`
  forms the product from uncentred logs. Centred, log sigma_e goes from
  **-0.378 [-0.596, -0.084]** to **+0.168 [+0.001, +0.307]**, same fit, same R2.
  `main.tex:905` and `:1380-1383` and the mediation table all print the
  uncentred sign. frag's +0.31 is unaffected.
- **The recovery bootstrap conditions on a positive denominator.**
  `analyse_boba_adaptations.py:270-289` drops resamples whose cost is <= 0 and
  prints the survivors' percentiles as a 95% interval. 68 of about 200 cells lose
  draws, one keeps 53 of 2000. Nine rows in `adaptations_recovery.csv` and 18 in
  the end-of-study table print a finite interval for a ratio whose point estimate
  is undefined.
- **Table 25 pools a different magnitude grid per arm.** The eight spike rows are
  0.25 sigma onset 0 only; the schedule rows are 1 and 5 sigma onset 0; the
  acquisition rows are all four magnitudes. Since the aggregate is a ratio of
  means, arms that have 5 sigma cells are dominated by them. On a shared subgrid
  the ranking reorders: augmented EI +55.5% at 0.25 sigma against the re-anchored
  cap's +69.5%. The preamble states landscapes, seeds and onsets and is silent on
  magnitudes.
- **The multi-objective rows aggregate raw hypervolume.**
  `analyse_boba_adaptations.py:256` falls through `opt_z.get(d, 1.0)` because no
  MO problem has an opt_z. vehiclesafety carries 90.6% of the denominator, so the
  halo backfit's "+25%" is one problem. Per-problem the four values are +37%, +1%,
  +23%, +51%.
- **The fitted-oracle row has no normalisation and three clusters.** Its printed
  interval `[-3504, -49]` is exactly the min and max of the three per-dataset
  ratios. A three-cluster bootstrap cannot say anything else, and the Wilcoxon
  floor at n = 3 is p = 0.25.
- **"29 to 38%" is produced by nothing.** Every fitted-to-analytic ratio in
  `fitted_vs_synthetic.csv` is 4.9, 23.8, 33.6, 31.5 at onset 0 and 23.6, 44.1,
  57.7, 50.2 at onset 21. No 29, no 29 to 38. `main.tex:794`.
- **"About a third" is one of three datasets.** fitted at 1 sigma onset 0 is
  opticarvis 0.052, provoice 0.192, ehmi 0.753. Drop ehmi and the fitted design
  reports an eighth. The published CI is again the min and the max.
- **The elicitation arm's drift, bias and ceiling carry no rating noise.**
  `elicitation_compare.py:118-126` returns zeros for all three. The sweep's drift
  adds gaussian jitter on top of the ramp, and its own comment says a review
  rejected the ramp-only variant for being "the only deterministic, jitter-free
  condition". A noise-free monotone ramp is exactly what a comparison is immune to
  by construction. The comment at `:113` claims "as in the sweep" and is false.
  The paper discloses this for bias only. Affects `main.tex:163-164`.
  Same arm: SPIKE was never run, yet the appendix concludes about gross faults;
  "ten of the 960 fits fell back" uses the run count, the fit count is 14,880.
- **Table 4's "absolute loss" is a search metric captioned as deployment.**
  `ABSOLUTE_METRIC = auc_simple_regret_true_postonset_per_iter_jitter`
  (`analyse_boba_robustness.py:81`) is the noisy run's best-visited regret, time
  averaged. `main.tex:466` calls it "the deployment number". The body two
  paragraphs later contradicts the caption.
- **`main.tex:284` divides a final value by a trajectory average.** "At 5 sigma it
  is 71.1% against 22.0%, a factor of 3.2". Like for like the factor is 2.50 at 5
  sigma and 1.72 at 1 sigma.
- **Stars in the adaptations table use a different rule from the script.** The
  table stars on "the interval excludes zero", the script on a BH-corrected
  Wilcoxon. Two of thirteen stars fail the script's own test, one on a bootstrap
  lower bound of +0.000395.
- **Two per-condition recoveries quoted in the text fail their own FDR.**
  `main.tex:1845` (18% [5,29], q = 0.137) and `main.tex:1855-1857` (76% [27,245],
  q = 0.129). Each is the largest of its eight-cell family. The negative
  companions quoted in the same sentences do survive.
- **The detector comparison is degenerate.** The residual z is not standard
  normal: rms per landscape runs 0.59 to 11.40, and the tuned CUSUM threshold is
  h = 3455. CUSUM and GLR fire on the same 6,599 of 6,600 held-out runs, so
  "the CUSUM matches the classical optimum" is an arithmetic identity. The common
  false-alarm budget holds only pooled: 0.0% on branin against 28.8% on shekel.
- **The free ship-rule change is not worthless.** `budget_split.py:319` reads
  `regret_lcb1` while the paper's cautious rule is mean minus two SDs. On gaussian
  at 0.25 sigma and above, lcb2 buys +0.046 with no extra trials, the size of the
  tournament headline. `analyse_ship_rules.py`'s own output already says so.
- **The two figure defects of 18 September.** The decomposition figure adds a
  0.004 spacer to the bar bottom, so the drawn total exceeds the data by 27% at
  0.05 sigma onset 1 and 52% at onset 21. The frag scatter drops 9 of 80 cells,
  eight with frag exactly zero and one where the measured cost is negative;
  Spearman is 0.871 on the kept 71 and 0.839 on all 80.

## D. Latent, nothing wrong in the shipped data

- **The clean-run guard covers five of the ten settings that change the clean run.**
  `bo_synthetic_error_simulation.py:494-514` tracks min_distance, input_uncertain,
  relevance_pursuit, rater_model and mo_halo_model. It does not track
  replicate_first, final_rerate, nigp, `inference_rule=best_mean` or student_t,
  and with no tracked settings the guard returns early. A `--resume` of such an
  arm into `output-boba` would silently reuse the standard clean baseline. The
  replicating clean run is 0.667 worse than the standard clean run against a total
  error cost of 0.416, so the arm's excess would come out sign-flipped.
  `bo_sensor_error_simulation.py` has no guard at all and puts no arm string in
  the baseline filename, so two arms' baselines are byte-identical in name.
- **`score_policies` averages with `groupby().mean()`,** which skips NaN. With only
  the default k-sweep directory loaded, 1,751 of 12,800 derived scores are NaN and
  the derived policy would be averaged over an 86% subsample while the standard
  keeps every run. The shipped run pooled both directories and has no NaN.
- **Diagnostic heatmaps average over `variant`,** collapsing four spike severities
  into one cell. The CSVs are split correctly; only the PNGs are wrong, and none
  is in the paper.
- **`evaluation_report.txt` states the opposite of what the FDR code does.**
- **The 23 budget-neutral numbers exist in no artefact.** `--arms` truncates and
  rewrites `adaptations_recovery.csv`, and the shipped file predates the sweep.
  All 23 reproduce, but not from anything in the repository.
- **Extra-trial censoring.** `analyse_extra_runs.py:143` returns `T - origin`, not
  `T - k` as the docstring says, so the censoring floor varies per run and
  "every median is exact" does not follow. Kaplan-Meier agrees with the plug-in
  median to within a trial where it exists; at 5 sigma, 70% censored, it does not
  exist and the table prints a bound as if it were a median.

## E. Checked and clean, with the evidence

- **Pairing.** Inner join on dataset, objective, acquisition, seed, oracle model,
  onset and budget, `validate="many_to_one"`, with a raise on duplicate baselines.
  Across all 46 output directories the paired rows per cell equal the variant
  count exactly. `output-boba` has 3,840 paired rows per benchmark and zero drops.
- **Seeding.** Trials 1 to 20 verified bit-identical between noisy and clean twins
  for 380 onset-20 runs across 11 arms. All four corruption channels return before
  drawing when the iteration is at or before the onset.
- **The decomposition identity** holds to 1e-6 at every cell, selection loss on
  clean runs is exactly zero, and two independently written pipelines agree on the
  deployed excess to better than 5e-4.
- **Units.** A full sweep of every likelihood-noise, standardised-residual and
  posterior site found no second instance of the A2 bug and no double conversion.
- **Time bases.** The post-onset window is defined in exactly one place and
  applied identically to both sides of every pair and to both onsets.
- **The headline selection share** recomputes to 0.4184 [0.336, 0.507] against the
  script's [0.341, 0.504], and the 61.8% pool reproduces exactly.
- **The multi-objective arm** is clean on every question asked: the reference
  point is fixed within a pair, the denominator is handled explicitly, the
  headroom screen is consistent, and all counts reproduce.
- **Prices are against the standard clean run.** For every reported arm the
  reference's clean run is bit-identical to `output-boba`'s.
- **The acquisition ranking is balanced**: all twelve have exactly 640 cells and
  ten seeds, and within-condition BH is more conservative than one family.
- **No undisclosed leakage in any replay.** Oracle and ceiling rows are labelled
  in the code. Detector tuning and scoring seeds are disjoint. Replays reproduce
  the logged prefix to 1e-14.
- **The 76,800 paired runs** are exactly 20 x 12 x 4 x 2 x 4 x 10.
- **All four figures** land on pages 5 to 9 and reproduce their tables to four
  decimals.
