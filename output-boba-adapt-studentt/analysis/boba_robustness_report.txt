2. WHAT MAKES AN ERROR 'BIG'?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Power law E[excess] = A * sigma_e^b_c * opt_z^b_z on landscape x magnitude
  cell means: quasi-Poisson estimating equations, which keep cells whose mean
  excess is <= 0, with BCa intervals from resampling benchmarks. SPREAD predicts
  b_z = 0; GAIN predicts b_c + b_z - 1 = 0 (the 'gain gap'). log-OLS refits
  log(excess) on the positive cells only, as a check on the estimator.
