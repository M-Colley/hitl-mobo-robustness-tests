ROBUSTNESS EVALUATION REPORT
================================================================================

Recommended primary evaluation
- Report BOTH excess regret (robustness) and absolute noisy-run regret
  (deployment quality): excess regret alone can crown acquisitions that
  are uniformly bad in baseline and noisy conditions alike.
- Lower excess AUC simple regret is better (within one condition).
- For ANY comparison across jitter onsets use the post-onset,
  per-noisy-iteration excess AUC; whole-run AUC scales with the window.
- Inference regret (the *_inference_* metrics) scores the design the
  experimenter would actually pick from noisy observations; it is the
  deployment-relevant recommendation quality.
- Response-step excess L2 is descriptive only.

Data coverage
- Response rows: 40
- Paired baseline/jitter rows: 20
- Seeds per condition: min=10, median=10.0, max=10
- Acquisition-optimization fallbacks in jittered runs: 0

Statistical notes
- FDR (Benjamini-Hochberg): ONE family covers every condition-level omnibus
  test in the table below (Friedman for k>2 acquisitions, Wilcoxon signed-rank
  for k=2). The noisy-vs-clean effect-size p-values (t and Wilcoxon, in
  effect_sizes_cohens_dz.csv) are corrected per column, each as one family.
- Kendall's W accompanies every Friedman test as the effect size.
- Dataset comparisons should stay stratified by dataset rather than pooling
  unless outcomes are explicitly normalized across datasets.
