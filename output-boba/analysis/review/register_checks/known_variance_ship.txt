Gains over the standard process (the best single rating) on the deployed design at T = 50, in units of opt_z;
price = regret added to the clean twin; recovered = gain / cost, a ratio of landscape means with a landscape
bootstrap; 'no share' where the cost is below 0.01 of opt_z or negative on a landscape (the gain is then the
result). 'vs learned' is the paired advantage over the same rule after the learned-noise refit. p is a
two-sided Wilcoxon over the per-landscape values. Post hoc, outside the paper's Holm families; Holm values
below are over the eight cells of one level only. LogEI and qNEI unless the scope says ten acquisitions.
