Re-rating with memory: the final sitting and the confirmation trials when the rater remembers (post hoc).

POST HOC, outside every Holm family of the paper; the memory strengths rho_m are assumed. Model: a look at a design whose original rating had error e_orig gets rho_m * e_orig + sqrt(1 - rho_m^2) * e_fresh, e_fresh the published replay's own draw for that look, e_orig = objective_observed - objective_true of the design's original rating in the run log (a design rated more than once before the procedure: its highest rating, ties to the earliest, the rating the standard ship rule reads). Noisy runs only: a clean twin's original errors are zero and its looks exact, so its shipped design and every price are unchanged by construction (checked run by run in the sitting; the confirmation's clean-twin claims are the published ones).

Control (i): at rho_m = 0 every one of 3200 noisy runs (and its clean twin) ships the published sitting (tournament_k2_lcb_rho1_look of end_of_study_ksweep, tournament_k16_lcb_rho1_look of end_of_study_kwide) bit for bit: largest difference 0.0e+00 against the replay's per-stem outputs and 0.0e+00 against end_of_study_per_run.csv.gz after the replay's own CSV round trip (without it 1812 runs differ by at most 7.1e-15, the published table's float parsing). Units: shares of the achievable improvement opt_z, deployed design at T = 50. gain = standard process less the sitting with memory (noisy run); price = the sitting less the standard process on the clean twin (unchanged by memory); 'over memoryless' = the published sitting's regret less the regret with memory, per run (negative: memory costs the sitting); 'over LCB1'/'over LCB2' = the regret of LCB1/LCB2 after all 50 trials (no extra trial) less the regret with memory. [95% landscape bootstrap, 2,000 resamples; the landscapes share their random numbers, so it treats correlated clusters as independent]; ahd = landscapes of 20 with a positive value; p = two-sided Wilcoxon over the 20 landscape means; Holm = the six (cell, rho_m > 0) rows of (i), per seed set and estimand (rho_m = 0, the published sitting, stands outside); share of cost = gain over the standard process's cost of error, a ratio of landscape means, withheld where that cost is below 0.01 or negative on a landscape; moved = share of noisy runs whose shipped design differs from the published sitting's. The intervals of the rho_m = 0 rows come from this script's bootstrap and differ in the third decimal from the paper's generator.
