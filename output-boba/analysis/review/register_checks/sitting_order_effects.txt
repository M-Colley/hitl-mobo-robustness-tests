Structural notes (algebra, not findings). With two designs, anchoring cannot reverse the comparison: y_1 = (1 - a)(t_1 + e_1) + a y_0 exceeds y_0 exactly when t_1 + e_1 does, so the sitting of two is unchanged by it in every run. With two designs, contrast ships the second design when (1 + c)(t_1 - t_0) > e_0 - e_1 in either order: it scales the true difference by 1 + c, so as specified (on the true values, not on the previous rating) it can only sharpen a comparison of two. With more designs it also favours a design shown just after a poor one, which is what its price on the clean twins measures. Fatigue scales only the look error, so it is inert on a clean twin and its price is the published sitting's.
